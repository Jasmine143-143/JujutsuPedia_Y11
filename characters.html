Suguru Geto

Grade: Special Grade

Affiliation: Formerly Jujutsu High

Role: Curse User

Personality: Intelligent, calm, confident and caring towards people he considers important.

Background: Geto was once a student at Jujutsu High and was one of Gojo's closest friends. After becoming disillusioned with the world of jujutsu, he left the school and became a curse user.

Techniques

  1. Cursed Spirit Manipulation
  2. Maximum: Uzumaki
  3. Cursed Spirit Absorption
  4. Cursed Spirit Extraction

Satoru Gojo

Grade: Special Grade

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer / Teacher

Personality: Confident, playful, carefree and often jokes around, but becomes serious when protecting others.

Background: Gojo was born into the famous Gojo family and inherited both the Limitless technique and the Six Eyes. He became a teacher at Jujutsu High and wants to help create a better future for young sorcerers.

Techniques

  1. Limitless
  2. Six Eyes
  3. Reverse Cursed Technique
  4. Hollow Purple
  5. Unlimited Void

Shoko Ieiri

Grade: Grade 1 Sorcerer

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Doctor / Jujutsu Sorcerer

Personality: Calm, laid-back, practical and usually unbothered by the situations around her.

Background: Shoko was a student at Jujutsu High alongside Gojo and Geto. She later became the school's doctor and uses her knowledge of Reverse Cursed Technique to treat injured sorcerers.

Techniques

  1. Reverse Cursed Technique
  2. Healing
  3. Cursed Energy
  4. Medical Expertise

Yuji Itadori

Grade: Grade 1 Sorcerer

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer / Vessel

Personality: Kind, energetic, brave and caring towards others.

Background: Yuji was originally an ordinary high school student with incredible physical strength. His life changed after he became the vessel of Ryomen Sukuna.

Techniques

  1. Superhuman Strength
  2. Black Flash
  3. Divergent Fist
  4. Blood Manipulation
  5. Shrine

Megumi Fushiguro

Grade: Grade 2 Sorcerer

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer

Personality: Quiet, serious and reserved, but deeply cares about the people close to him.

Background: Megumi is a student at Jujutsu High and comes from the Zenin family. He inherited the powerful Ten Shadows Technique.

Techniques

  1. Ten Shadows Technique
  2. Divine Dogs
  3. Nue
  4. Max Elephant
  5. Chimera Shadow Garden

Nobara Kugisaki

Grade: Grade 3 Sorcerer

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer

Personality: Confident, outspoken, independent and determined.

Background: Nobara comes from a small rural town and moved to Tokyo to attend Jujutsu High. She wanted to experience life in the city while becoming a sorcerer.

Techniques

  1. Straw Doll Technique
  2. Resonance
  3. Hairpin
  4. Black Flash

Yuta Okkotsu

Grade: Special Grade

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer

Personality: Kind, polite and caring, but becomes very serious when protecting others.

Background: Yuta was haunted by the cursed spirit of his childhood friend Rika. He later joined Jujutsu High and learned to control his cursed energy.

Techniques

  1. Copy
  2. Rika
  3. Reverse Cursed Technique
  4. Black Flash
  5. Domain Expansion

Maki Zenin

Grade: Grade 1 Sorcerer

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer

Personality: Strong-willed, determined, independent and competitive.

Background: Maki was born into the Zenin clan but was treated poorly because she had very little cursed energy. She trained herself to become a powerful fighter.

Abilities

  1. Heavenly Restriction
  2. Weapon Mastery
  3. Enhanced Physical Strength
  4. Enhanced Speed
  5. Exceptional Combat Skills

Ryomen Sukuna

Grade: Special Grade / King of Curses

Affiliation: Himself

Role: Curse

Personality: Cruel, arrogant, confident and extremely selfish.

Background: Sukuna is known as the King of Curses. He existed over a thousand years ago and became one of the most feared beings in jujutsu history.

Techniques

  1. Dismantle
  2. Cleave
  3. Malevolent Shrine
  4. Fire Technique
  5. Reverse Cursed Technique

Mahito

Grade: Special Grade Curse

Affiliation: Disaster Curses

Role: Cursed Spirit

Personality: Playful, curious, manipulative and extremely dangerous.

Background: Mahito is a cursed spirit born from humans' hatred and fear of one another. He is fascinated by the human soul and enjoys experimenting with his abilities.

Techniques

  1. Idle Transfiguration
  2. Self-Transfiguration
  3. Polymorphic Soul Isomer
  4. Black Flash
  5. Self-Embodiment of Perfection

Kenjaku

Grade: Special Grade Threat

Affiliation: Curse User / Cursed Spirits

Role: Ancient Sorcerer / Manipulator

Personality: Intelligent, patient, manipulative and curious about jujutsu.

Background: Kenjaku is an ancient sorcerer who has lived for over a thousand years by transferring his brain into different bodies.

Techniques

  1. Body Swapping
  2. Cursed Spirit Manipulation
  3. Antigravity System
  4. Reverse Cursed Technique
  5. Domain Expansion

Kento Nanami

Grade: Grade 1 Sorcerer

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer / Teacher

Personality: Calm, serious, responsible and practical.

Background: Nanami was a former student of Jujutsu High who left the jujutsu world to work as an office employee. He later returned to become a sorcerer and help younger students.

Techniques

  1. Ratio Technique
  2. Overtime
  3. Collapse
  4. Black Flash

Toji Fushiguro

Grade: No Cursed Energy

Affiliation: Independent

Role: Sorcerer Killer

Personality: Confident, calm, ruthless and independent.

Background: Toji was born into the Zenin family but had almost no cursed energy. Because of his Heavenly Restriction, he developed incredible physical abilities and became a highly skilled assassin.

Abilities

  1. Heavenly Restriction
  2. Enhanced Physical Strength
  3. Enhanced Speed
  4. Weapon Mastery
  5. Inverted Spear of Heaven

Riko Amanai

Grade: Star Plasma Vessel

Affiliation: Jujutsu High / Master Tengen

Role: Star Plasma Vessel

Personality: Cheerful, energetic, confident and friendly.

Background: Riko was chosen as a Star Plasma Vessel for Master Tengen. Gojo and Geto were assigned to protect her while she travelled to the Tombs of the Star Corridor.

Abilities

  1. Star Plasma Vessel
  2. Cursed Energy
  3. Swimming
  4. Quick Thinking

Panda

Grade: Semi-Grade 2

Affiliation: Jujutsu High

Role: Cursed Corpse / Student

Personality: Friendly, playful, caring and supportive towards his friends.

Background: Panda is a unique cursed corpse created by Masamichi Yaga. Despite his appearance, he is intelligent and can think and act like a human.

Abilities

  1. Three Cores
  2. Gorilla Mode
  3. Panda Mode
  4. Physical Strength
  5. Cursed Energy

Hiromi Higuruma

Grade: Grade 1 Sorcerer

Affiliation: Culling Game

Role: Jujutsu Sorcerer / Lawyer

Personality: Intelligent, serious, determined and strongly focused on justice.

Background: Higuruma was originally a defence lawyer who became involved in the Culling Game. After awakening his cursed technique, he became a powerful jujutsu sorcerer.

Techniques

  1. Deadly Sentencing
  2. Judgeman
  3. Confiscation
  4. Domain Expansion
  5. Reverse Cursed Technique

Naoya Zenin

Affiliation: Zenin Clan

Background: Naoya is a member of the Zenin clan and the son of Naobito Zenin. He was considered a talented sorcerer and believed he was destined to become the head of the Zenin clan.
